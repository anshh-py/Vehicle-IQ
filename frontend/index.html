WHAT IT CAN — AND CANNOT — TELL YOU

Evidence first.
Always.

Useful signals

  • Text recognized in photos, for you to verify
  • Visible image findings with available confidence and evidence
  • A research-only estimate when details and a model are available
  • A recommendation based on stored results and asking price, when supplied

Important limits

  • Photo review cannot certify a vehicle as damage-free
  • Text read from photos does not confirm identity or document authenticity
  • Model estimates are not verified market value or sale prices
  • Missing evidence and unavailable services stay clearly unavailable

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently
asked.

Do I need every vehicle detail to start?

No. Begin with photos. The page asks for specifications only if the a price estimate needs them.

Does a price estimate mean the asking price is fair?

No. A configured research model estimate is separate from market valuation. Without sufficient permitted comparable evidence, market valuation is unavailable.

Can this replace an independent inspection?

No. Use the review to prepare questions, then inspect the car and its documents in person with a qualified professional.

Why might part of a review be unavailable?

Image quality, missing details or unavailable services can limit results. The report identifies gaps rather than filling them in.
